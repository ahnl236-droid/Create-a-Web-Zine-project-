PERSONAL RECORDENTRY 05 / 14

I don’t have a specific lucky charm to call lucky, but if I had to choose one for the sake of this assignment it’d probably be a lion necklace I usually wear. I got it at some thrift store in Hong Kong for $10 USD (80HKD) in 2023 and never wore it since it looked kinda chunky and just this thing hanging around my neck. It’s a gold-plated necklace of a lion head with a thin chain.

I think ever since I moved to New York for school it’s been the only necklace I wear. Not that it has much sentimental value or grants me luck, but I’ve grown to like the clunkiness of it and the connotations of a lion. But since I wear it almost everyday it’s starting to wear out, and the gold (/gold-looking) plating is coming off and you can see some cooper-y patches underneath.

I try my best to take care of it (as in not getting it near water), but over time it’s inevitable it’ll start wearing out, which is kinda sad I guess. Hoping to somehow eternalize it. It doesn’t bring me luck, but wearing it makes me feel like I have a little lion guardian guarding me at all times — like those Chinese guardian lion statues at the front of buildings. If I were to be really superstitious, this little necklace wards off evil spirits for me. It has taken care of me well and only good things have happened since.
