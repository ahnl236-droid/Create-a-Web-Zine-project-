PERSONAL RECORDENTRY 07 / 14

My lucky charm is my mom’s necklace, which I take with me everywhere. It’s a delicate silver and copper chain with an opalite stone that catches the light in a way I like. To anyone else it would look like ordinary jewelry, but to me it’s more than that. I live away from my family, so I miss small things, like having my mom around when I need her. Wearing her necklace is a simple way of keeping a piece of home with me. It reminds me that she’s still in my corner, even when she isn’t nearby. I feel like its lucky for me, and having something familiar with me helps when I’m nervous or overwhelmed. It’s a small, steady reminder that things usually work out. So for me it’s less about luck and more about having something that keeps me connected to my family and home.
