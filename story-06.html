PERSONAL RECORDENTRY 06 / 14

My lucky charm(s) are omamori, a Japanese amulet you can get from Shinto shrines and Buddhist temples. It's a little charm, typically consisting of a fabric pouch, with a sacred paper or wooden slip inside. I have multiple, usually one per bag—in my schoolbag I have a white omamori from the Izumo Oyashiro shrine, a small beige omamori from Susa shrine in my everyday bag, a pink and white omamori from Kanda shrine in my totebag, etc... It's primarily for luck and protection, but also a small, physical piece of home I can carry around with me to places unknown.
